SYNTHETIC NOTE FOR RESEARCH. NOT A REAL PATIENT.

A 71-year-old woman in the emergency department has 2 days of new shortness of breath and pleuritic chest pain. She has left calf swelling and tenderness along the deep veins, and has been bed-bound for 10 days after an acute lumbar disc herniation. She has a heart rate of 112 beats/min, an oxygen saturation of 92% on room air, no hemoptysis, no history of cancer, and no prior DVT or PE. The clinician judges pulmonary embolism to be the leading explanation, with no alternative more likely. A CT pulmonary angiogram shows a filling defect in a pulmonary artery.
